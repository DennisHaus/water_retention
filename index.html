RAINWATER
RETENTION
SIMULATOR

A simplified topography-based model for visualising rainfall, surface flow, runoff and water retention in depressions and behind artificial dams.

For visualisation, comparison and design exploration only.

MODEL DESCRIPTION
RAINFALL Rainfall is applied uniformly over the entire projected topographic area. One litre per square metre equals one millimetre of rainfall.
SURFACE FLOW Flowlines follow an interpolated downhill terrain gradient. Their geometry is simplified and smoothed for visual clarity. Flowlines stop when they would loop back across themselves.
RETENTION Rainfall entering a depression is retained until it reaches its spill elevation. Excess water is passed to a downstream basin or counted as runoff.
TOPOGRAPHY PLY, STL and OBJ models are sampled into a regular heightfield. Each horizontal cell has one terrain elevation.
ANALYSIS LAYERS Optional layers show approximate slope orientation, slope steepness and watershed catchments.
LIMITATIONS This is not an engineering flood model. It does not calculate infiltration, evaporation, groundwater, soil permeability, drainage infrastructure, sediment transport or flow velocity.
EXPORT Retained-water exports use the cell-based volumetric water form represented in the viewer. Flowlines and analysis layers can also be exported for comparison and further visualisation.
