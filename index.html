RAINWATER
RETENTION
SIMULATOR

A simplified topography-based model for visualising rainfall, surface flow, runoff and water retention in depressions and behind artificial dams.

For visualisation, comparison and design exploration only.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION
MODEL DESCRIPTION
RAINFALL Rainfall is applied uniformly over the complete projected topographic area. The input uses litres per square metre, equivalent to millimetres of rainfall. One L/m² equals one mm.
RETENTION BASINS Depressions are identified from the terrain drainage network. Basins smaller than 5% of the capacity of the largest basin are neglected in the retention calculation. Rainfall entering the retained basins is filled up to their spill elevation.
SPILL AND RUNOFF When a basin reaches its spill elevation, excess water is routed to the next significant downstream basin where possible. Remaining water is counted as runoff or spill through the open terrain boundary.
WATER GEOMETRY Retained-water geometry follows the sampled terrain cells. Each wet cell is clipped against the water level, so the water volume follows sloping terrain edges rather than being a simple vertical extrusion.
FLOWLINES Flowlines follow a continuously interpolated downhill gradient. They are deliberately simplified visual paths. Flowlines are smoothed, extended across the terrain where possible and stopped if they approach a retained basin or would cross themselves.
SLOPE ORIENTATION The slope-orientation layer displays arrows pointing in the approximate downhill direction of the terrain surface.
SLOPE STEEPNESS The slope-steepness layer uses a colour gradient from blue for low slopes to red for steep slopes. The legend uses a range of 0° to 45° or more.
WATERSHEDS The watershed layer shows the drainage catchments associated with the significant basins. Their boundaries are interpolated over three neighbouring points to reduce the appearance of the underlying raster structure.
MODEL SCALE Imported PLY, STL and OBJ files do not reliably contain unit metadata. Set model scale and metres per model unit manually. The importer assumes Y is elevation and X/Z form the horizontal plane.
LIMITATIONS This is not an engineering flood model. It does not calculate infiltration, evaporation, groundwater, soil permeability, drainage infrastructure, sediment transport, water velocity or flood safety.
EXPORTS Water volumes can be exported as PLY or STL. Flowlines and slope orientation can be exported as OBJ. Slope steepness and watershed layers can be exported as coloured PLY files. Viewer images and retention tables can also be downloaded.
