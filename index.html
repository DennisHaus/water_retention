RAINWATER
RETENTION
SIMULATOR

A simplified topography-based model for visualising how rainfall is retained in depressions, basins and artificial dams.

For visualisation, comparison and design exploration only.

MODEL DESCRIPTION
INPUT Rainfall is entered as litres per square metre. One litre per square metre is equivalent to one millimetre of rainfall.
TOPOGRAPHY The model uses a regular heightfield. Uploaded PLY, STL and OBJ models are sampled into one elevation value per horizontal cell.
RETENTION MODEL Rainfall is treated as surface runoff that can collect in topographic depressions. Each depression is filled until its lowest spill point. Water leaving the terrain boundary or exceeding the available retention capacity is counted as runoff or spill.
VOLUME Retained water volume is calculated from the water depth in each terrain cell multiplied by the horizontal cell area.
LIMITATIONS This model does not calculate infiltration, evaporation, groundwater, soil permeability, flow velocity, pipe drainage, sediment transport or engineering flood behaviour.
UPLOADED DAMS Artificial dams must be represented in the uploaded topography. Very narrow dams may disappear if the terrain resolution is too low.
